01 / Entrepreneurship2026
GiVV

Rooted in giving.

$15,000Startup competition award

Co-founder & COO

Making local giving
feel personal.

A social marketplace concept connecting Tulane students with local nonprofits through micro-donations, social sharing, and student organization competitions.

Inside the project +

The challenge

Students want to contribute, but limited funds, trust, and decision fatigue can get in the way.

The approach

Our team conducted 15+ interviews to understand what motivates giving. We shaped the concept around personal connection, friends’ activity, and simple payments. As co-founder and COO, I focused on strategy and operations alongside my teammates.

The result

GiVV won first place and a $15,000 award at Tulane’s Strategy Start-Up Lab final pitch in April 2026.

  • Social impact
  • User research
  • Operations
